# Home surface

Mode: Read. Selected 12ui candidate D, computing-journal direction. Product brief and user continuation authorise implementation decisions.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A compact computing journal whose news begins immediately. No marketing hero between readers and source-linked information.

OWN-WORLD: Warm paper, near-black ink and saturated orange; condensed oversized masthead; thin rules; topic rail; black reading-list panel; restrained geometric engineering artwork.

STORY: Discover a relevant headline, understand its practical impact, follow the original source, save it or discuss it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width masthead and search, narrow topic rail on the left, two-part feature in the centre, saved/community rail on the right. Four compact stories continue below the lead. Primary action is opening the lead story; original source remains directly accessible.

FORM: Computing-journal workbench. Grounded alternatives considered: developer documentation index, independent magazine, release ledger, community noticeboard, computing journal, research abstract index, workshop parts catalogue. Impeccable seed a53aefa9 assigned fifth; 12ui D embodies that direction. Catalog challengers did not improve both identification and clarity: broadside density informs compact coverage; generative identity contributes coherent geometric branding; technical zine reinforces explanation; toy and surreal forms would obscure news discovery. The selected screen is the visual reference, not the fabricated text it contains.

Signature interaction: saving a story immediately updates the reading-list rail. Motion is limited to small state transitions; reduced motion remains static. Headline width and size adapt to real copy.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
