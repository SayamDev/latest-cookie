# Daily discovery surfaces

Mode: Operate / Read. Code-led extension of the existing computing journal; keep DESIGN.md.

## Intent
Reach fresh outlet reporting, discover recent tech videos by topic, and navigate a large model catalogue without endless scrolling. News is linked metadata, videos are selected-channel feeds, popularity signals have explicit scope.

## Own-world
Inherit paper/ink/orange, ruled layouts, condensed headings, serif headlines and square controls. No new branding, illustrations or decorative dashboard cards.

## Story and first viewport
Homepage shows a compact news desk before editorial picks, with direct routes to news, Watch and Model Lab. News page opens with source freshness and search/outlet controls then headline rows. Watch leads with an AI topic selector, channel/window/order controls and real thumbnails. Model Lab retains chart/calculator and adds newer-first sorting, pricing-route filters, incremental results and an anchored trend/speed section.

## Form and signature interaction
The topic row switches the video collection immediately, with pressed states and explicit empty results. Most-viewed/newest sorting changes actual items. No claim to reproduce personalized YouTube Home. Failed refresh preserves content and says so. Feed health exposes per-source last-success times.

## Proof
Parser fixtures, unit tests, desktop/mobile Playwright flows, light/dark axe scans and rendered screenshots. Manual screen reader/Safari/Firefox remain outside tested coverage.
